# Sound credits

All sounds of CivSim 2 in this folder were made for the project by `tools/audio/make_audio.py` (2026-11-24):

- **Music** (`music/`) and the short pieces in `sfx/` (`era_up`, `research_done`, `victory`, `defeat`, `war_declared`,
  `peace`, `settlement_founded`, `alert_danger`, `alert_notice`): notes composed by the script, played with the
  **FluidR3 GM** sound font by Frank Wen, released under the **MIT licence** (Copyright © 2000-2002, 2008 Frank Wen,
  © 2008 Toby Smithe), rendered with FluidSynth.
- **Ambient loops** (`ambient/`) and the other effects in `sfx/`: synthesized from noise and sine waves by the script,
  no recordings.

They belong to the project; no attribution is required by any of them beyond this note for the sound font.
