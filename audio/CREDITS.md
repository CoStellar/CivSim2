# Sound credits

All sounds of CivSim 2 in this folder were made for the project by `tools/audio/make_audio.py` (2026-11-24):

- **Music** (`music/*.mid`): notes composed by the script (`tools/audio/styles.py`, `music.py`), played by the game on
  `instruments.sf2` with **MeltySynth** by Nobuaki Tanaka (MIT licence).
- **`instruments.sf2`**: the **FluidR3 GM** sound font by Frank Wen, cut down to the instruments the music uses
  (`tools/audio/trim_sf2.py`), released under the **MIT licence** (Copyright © 2000-2002, 2008 Frank Wen, © 2008 Toby Smithe).
- **Short pieces** in `sfx/` (`era_up`, `research_done`, `victory`, `defeat`, `war_declared`, `peace`,
  `settlement_founded`, `alert_danger`, `alert_notice`): notes composed by the script, played on FluidR3 GM with FluidSynth.
- **Ambient loops** (`ambient/`) and the other effects in `sfx/`: synthesized from noise and sine waves by the script,
  no recordings.

They belong to the project; the MIT licences of the sound font and of MeltySynth ask only for this note.
